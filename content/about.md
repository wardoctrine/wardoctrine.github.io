## What Is War Doctrine?

War Doctrine is an independent military history publication dedicated to the stories that didn't make it into the textbooks.

Not the famous battles. Not the generals everyone already knows. The decisions made under impossible pressure. The units history forgot. The moments where one choice — one person — changed the outcome of a war, a country, or a century.

Every story published here is researched from primary sources, academic records, and verified historical documentation. The goal is always the same: tell it straight, tell it accurately, and make you feel like you were there.

---

## Editorial Approach

War Doctrine covers military history from a documentary perspective. Every piece is:

- **Sourced** — drawn from historical records, museum archives, academic research, and verified accounts
- **Educational** — written to inform, not to sensationalize
- **Accurate** — factual claims are cross-referenced before publication
- **Balanced** — events are presented in their full historical context, without glorification of violence or extremism

Content covers conflicts across all eras and all nations. No side is celebrated. History is presented as it happened.

## The YouTube Channel

War Doctrine started as a YouTube Shorts channel — [@WarDoctrineHQ](https://youtube.com/@WarDoctrineHQ) — where each story is told in under 60 seconds. This website expands those stories into full long-form articles, with sources, context, and depth that a Short can't fit.

Think of the Shorts as the trailer. This site is the full film.
